2The Problem

Conflict-of-interest checks are mandatory before any new engagement — and today, they're still mostly manual.

Slow

30–60 minutes per check

A paralegal manually searches the conflicts database, old emails, and spreadsheets for every named party — repeated at every new stage of a deal.

Error-prone

Hidden structure gets missed

Entities rarely match the firm's old records exactly — subsidiaries, aliases, and UBOs are easy for a rushed keyword search to miss entirely.

High stakes

Severe cost of a miss

An undetected conflict can mean disqualification mid-deal, malpractice exposure, and lasting reputational damage.

Under pressure

Deadlines compound the risk

Deal teams are often asked to clear engagements within hours — exactly when careful review is hardest to guarantee.
